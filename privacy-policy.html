Easy Wine — Privacy Policy

Effective date: 15 September 2026

This policy explains what data the Easy Wine app handles and how. The app has no sign-up, no user accounts, and never asks for your name, email address or telephone number.

Who is responsible. The controller of the data described here is Aleksei Tarasov, the developer of Easy Wine. Contact: dev.alexey.tarasoff@gmail.com.

1. Data stored only on your device

Your taste preferences and budget, your scan history together with the shelf photographs in it, your favourites, and your language, theme and scanner settings are stored locally on your device. We do not receive or store them on our servers. They are removed when you delete the app.

2. Photographs of shelves, and the AI that reads them

When you scan, the photograph is sent through our server to Google Gemini, an artificial intelligence model, which reads the labels and returns what it recognised. Your taste preferences and budget are sent with it as part of the question, so that the answer can be ranked for you. The recommendations you see are generated by that AI.

We do not keep the photograph: our server passes it on and stores only the counters described in section 3. Which Gemini service handles it depends on where you are:

  • If your App Store country or your device region is in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland — or the app cannot tell — and for every subscriber, scans go to Google’s paid Gemini API service. Under its terms Google does not use them to improve its products.
  • Elsewhere, free scans may go to the free tier of the Gemini API, under whose terms Google may use the photographs and the questions sent with them to improve its models. This is also stated in the app before your first scan.

In both cases Google may keep them for a limited period to detect abuse, as its terms allow. Photograph a shelf, not people — and do not photograph anything you would not want processed this way.

3. Data our server stores

Against an anonymous identifier only — there is no account to attach it to:

  • An anonymous sign-in identifier, issued by Firebase Authentication. It carries no personal data and is replaced if you reinstall the app.
  • A device identifier derived on your device and stored as a hash. It is what lets a free allowance survive a reinstall, and what you can send us to be granted a complimentary subscription.
  • Counters: how many scans were made, and what they cost us to run. Numbers, not content.
  • Your subscription status: which product, when it expires, and Apple’s transaction identifier for it. See section 4.

Two further signals are sent with a scan and not stored against you: your App Store country and your device’s region setting, used only to choose the service described in section 2; and an app integrity token (Firebase App Check with Apple App Attest), which confirms the request comes from a genuine copy of the app and carries no personal data.

4. Purchases

Subscriptions are processed by Apple (App Store). We never see your payment details. What we receive is the signed receipt Apple issues, which we verify on our server so that a subscription cannot be faked; from it we store the product, the expiry date and Apple’s transaction identifier.

5. Diagnostics and usage

  • Firebase Crashlytics — crash and performance reports, so failures can be fixed.
  • Firebase Analytics — only if you turn on “Share usage statistics” in Settings; it is off until you do. Usage events (a scan started, a screen opened, a purchase completed), tied to a random identifier for this installation. Google derives an approximate location, at country and city level, from the network address; we neither request nor receive your device’s location. Turning the switch off stops collection at once and resets that identifier.

None of it is linked to your identity, and none of it is used for advertising or for tracking you across other companies’ apps and websites. The app contains no advertising and no advertising identifier.

6. Sharing

We do not sell your data. It is processed by Google — Firebase as our service provider, and Gemini as the recognition service described in section 2 — and by Apple for payments. No advertising networks are involved.

7. International transfers

Google and Apple may process this data outside the country where you live, including in the United States. Where the data comes from the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, those transfers rely on the providers’ certification under the EU–US Data Privacy Framework (and its UK and Swiss extensions) and on the Standard Contractual Clauses in their terms.

8. Legal bases

For people in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland: we process a photograph, your preferences and the counters in order to provide the scan you asked for and the subscription you bought (performance of a contract). The device identifier, the integrity check and the counters also serve our legitimate interest in preventing abuse of a service that costs money on every use, and crash reports serve our legitimate interest in keeping the app working. Usage statistics are collected only with your consent, given with the switch in Settings; you can withdraw it there at any time, which does not affect what was collected before.

The AI’s ranking is a suggestion about wine. It is not a decision that has legal or similarly significant effects on you.

9. Retention and deletion

Deleting the app removes everything held on your device. Server-side counters and the subscription record are kept against the anonymous identifier while the app is installed; diagnostics and analytics are retained according to Firebase’s own defaults. To have anything associated with your device deleted, send us the diagnostic code shown in Settings and write to dev.alexey.tarasoff@gmail.com.

10. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you have the right to access the data we hold about you, to have it corrected or deleted, to restrict or object to its processing, and to receive it in a portable form. Because we hold no name or email address, the diagnostic code shown in Settings is how we find what belongs to you — include it when you write. You also have the right to complain to the data protection authority in your country.

11. Age

Easy Wine is about alcoholic drinks and is rated for adults. It is not directed to children, and we do not knowingly collect their data.

12. Changes

We may update this policy; the effective date above will change accordingly, and a change that affects you materially will be announced in the app.

13. Contact
